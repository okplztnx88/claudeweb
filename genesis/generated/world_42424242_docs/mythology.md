# Mythology and Beliefs

## Cosmology

**Structure:** world tree connecting realms

### Cosmic Realms

**Count:** 8
**Connections:** sacred places as gateways

**Realms:**
- mortal world
- chaos beyond
- shadow realm
- realm of spirits
- dream world
- elemental plane
- primordial void
- heaven of the gods

### Celestial Bodies

**Sun:** divine being
**Moon:** partner of sun (Phases represent: birth, life, death cycle)
**Stars:** eternal fires
**Constellations:** 12 recognized patterns telling sacred stories

### Afterlife


**Type:** Multiple destinations
**Criteria:** based on burial rites

**Destinations:**
- reincarnation cycle
- void of forgetting


## Shared Myths




### The Golden Age

**Characteristics:**
- gods walked with mortals

**Outcome:** total destruction

**Triggers:**
